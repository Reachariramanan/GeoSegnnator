                    HOW TO PLACE NEGATIVE SEEDS - QUICK START
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT WAS THE PROBLEM?
─────────────────────
Your training was failing with: "Only one class in training labels (values=[0])"
This means the training mask had ONLY non-road pixels (all zeros).

Why? Because:
  • You were only placing POSITIVE (road) seeds
  • The algorithm needs BOTH road AND non-road examples to train

WHAT'S NEW?
───────────
✓ UI now has TWO buttons in the Seeds section:
  🟢 "+ Road"        (green) = Place positive seeds on roads
  🔴 "− Non-Road"    (red)   = Place negative seeds on non-roads

✓ Seeds are color-coded on the map:
  🟢 Green circle  = Positive seed (road)
  🔴 Red circle    = Negative seed (non-road)

✓ Sidebar shows seed type:
  ✓ r100, c200  = Positive seed
  ✗ r50, c400   = Negative seed

HOW TO USE IT:
──────────────
1. Check "Place seeds on click" ✓
2. Click "+ Road" (green button)
3. Click the map on ROAD pixels → green dots appear
4. Click "− Non-Road" (red button)
5. Click the map on NON-ROAD pixels (buildings, grass, water) → red dots appear
6. Run training with both types of seeds

IMPORTANT RULES:
────────────────
❌ DON'T: Place negative seeds in same region as positive seeds
✓ DO:    Place them spatially separated (different areas)

❌ DON'T: Place seeds only in one or two areas
✓ DO:    Spread seeds across different locations

❌ DON'T: Forget to adjust threshold if training still fails
✓ DO:    Try threshold values 0.3-0.6 before training

EXAMPLE PLACEMENT:
──────────────────
Road pixels:
  🟢 Seed 1 at highway center
  🟢 Seed 2 at local road
  🟢 Seed 3 at another highway

Non-road pixels:
  🔴 Seed 4 on building roof
  🔴 Seed 5 on vegetation/tree
  🔴 Seed 6 on bare soil

→ Now training will see both classes and work correctly!

TROUBLESHOOTING:
────────────────
Error: "Only one class in training labels"
├─ Cause 1: Negative seeds overlap with positive seeds in same connected region
│  └─ Fix: Place them far apart on the map
├─ Cause 2: Threshold is too aggressive
│  └─ Fix: Lower threshold slider (try 0.4-0.5)
└─ Cause 3: All seeds in same area
   └─ Fix: Spread seeds across entire map area
